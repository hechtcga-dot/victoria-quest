# Victoria's Quest

How to Be Less Annoying (in 3 Easy Levels). A browser adventure game.

Victoria starts at 100% annoying. Across three levels she talks to Thomas the dog, Jody the cat and Sharon the turtle, makes choices, and battles her sisters Lina and Grace, who each teach her a lesson. In the end she becomes the ultimate cool person.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser. No install, no build.

- Move: arrow keys or WASD (on-screen pad on phones)
- Talk / pick / next: SPACE, Enter, E, or tap
- Battles: cool moves need timing (press SPACE when the marker is in the green). Annoying moves do nothing and make your sister meaner.

## Files

- `index.html`: page and touch controls
- `game.js`: the whole game (levels, dialogue, battles, drawing). Story text lives in the `LEVELS` array.
